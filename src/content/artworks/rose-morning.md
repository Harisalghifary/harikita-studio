Nadia and Reza were married at eight in the morning, before the heat arrived.
The brief was one word: *morning*. So the palette stays low and warm, and the
illustration is framed like a window someone has just opened.

The roses are deliberately unfinished at the top edge — the piece is meant to
look like it continues past the paper.
