Alya and Dimas married in the last week of September, when the light goes
quiet. They asked for a single colour — the blue of a sea they had never
visited together — and one line of petals falling across the page.

The composition stays deliberately empty in the centre so the names have room
to breathe. Everything else is edge work: petals, a thin frame, a wash that
never quite resolves.
